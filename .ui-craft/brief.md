# Product purpose

MoviesFlix helps viewers discover movies and series, inspect seasons and episodes, and open their chosen title's watch, trailer, or download destination.

# Primary user

A viewer on a phone or desktop who is choosing something to watch and needs clear title and season details before opening it.

# Principles

1. Keep the existing burgundy and orange identity, local fonts, and cinematic imagery.
2. Use real title, season, and episode data; do not replace catalog content with decorative demo copy.
3. Keep the next useful action visible and route it through the existing links and provider behavior.
4. Treat dialogs as focused tasks: preserve keyboard access, Escape close, scroll containment, and focus return.
5. Motion explains a change of state, follows reduced-motion preferences, and never blocks an action.

# Success metric

The viewer can identify a title or season and reach its watch, trailer, or download action with a few direct interactions on both phone and desktop.

# Out of scope

- Changing the MoviesFlix brand palette, fonts, routes, or media provider URLs.
- Adding placeholder titles, seasons, episode details, or media images.
- Changing catalog queries or unrelated detail-page sections.
